Healthcare

Clinics, hospitals and diagnostic centers handle sensitive patient information on shared workstations, often with a small IT team.

Common risks

  • Shared logins on front-desk and ward computers
  • Patient records accessed from many devices
  • Connected medical and office equipment that is rarely updated
  • Ransomware that disrupts appointments and care

Professional Services

Law, accounting and consulting firms hold confidential client data and are frequent targets of email and payment fraud.

Common risks

  • Business email compromise and fake payment requests
  • Client files shared across many tools
  • Remote and hybrid staff on home networks
  • Client security questionnaires and audits

Education

Schools, colleges and training institutes manage large, constantly changing groups of users on limited budgets.

Common risks

  • Accounts that remain after students and staff leave
  • Many shared and personal devices
  • Public-facing portals and learning platforms
  • Phishing aimed at staff and students

Retail & Clinics

Multi-location stores and clinic chains run point-of-sale, booking and inventory systems across sites with little on-site IT.

Common risks

  • Payment and booking systems spread across locations
  • Devices set up once and rarely reviewed
  • Customer data held in cloud and third-party systems
  • Little visibility into what happens at each site

Real Estate

Brokerages and property managers handle high-value transactions and personal documents, often from phones and laptops on the move.

Common risks

  • Wire fraud and impersonation emails during transactions
  • Identity documents shared over email and messaging
  • Agents working from personal and mobile devices
  • Websites and listing portals exposed to the internet

Why it matters

Every device is
a way in.

A single unpatched laptop, a server with weak settings or an employee working from a café can give an attacker the foothold they need. Traditional antivirus catches known malware, but it rarely tells you when settings drift, files change unexpectedly or an account starts behaving strangely.

Endpoint security gives you continuous eyes on every device, so issues are found and handled before they spread.

  • Unknown devicesYou cannot protect what you do not know exists, and device lists go out of date quickly.
  • Weak configurationDefault settings, open services and missing hardening make devices easier to exploit.
  • Silent changesUnexpected changes to critical files or settings go unnoticed until something breaks.
  • Slow responseWithout alerts and a plan, one infected device can cause problems for days.

Common questions

Questions,
answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Does this replace our antivirus?

No. It works alongside the antivirus you already have, adding monitoring, configuration checks and response capabilities that antivirus alone does not provide. Where possible, we bring your antivirus alerts into the same view.

Will the agent slow down our computers?

The agent is designed to be lightweight and runs quietly in the background. We test on a small group of devices before rolling out to everyone.

What about staff who work from home?

Remote devices report over an encrypted connection, so they stay monitored wherever people work.

Which devices can you cover?

Windows, macOS and Linux computers and servers. For equipment such as firewalls and network devices, we collect their logs through our Visibility service.

What happens when something is detected?

You receive a clear alert explaining what happened and what to do. Simple, pre-approved responses can run automatically; anything bigger follows the escalation steps we agree with you up front.
